How to cut

  1. Set the gauge. Turn the wheel or type a number. The red line is where the blade drops; the lift's front edge sticks out past it.
  2. Jog the lift square against the gauge. Skip it and the cut wanders.
  3. Clamp, then hold both CUT buttons.
  4. Trim and finished cards sort themselves. Pieces with work left go to the tray. Rotate or turn the lift to reach other edges.
  5. Strips of the same size can go back in together with Add to lift, up to 3 1/2" high.
